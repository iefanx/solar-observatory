BEHIND THE OBSERVATORY

Real orbits. A wider perspective.

Built with Three.js and a GPU Kepler solver, inspired by Ian Webster’s Asterank. The asteroid catalogue is a bundled snapshot; planetary orbits use approximate J2000 elements. The Moon uses a locally bundled Astronomy Engine ephemeris.

Distances use astronomical units. Overview markers are enlarged for visibility; local Earth–Moon views use physical radii. Glow is artistic, not measured apparent brightness. Two-body asteroid and planet paths omit gravitational perturbations.

Earth’s spin follows simulation time. The Moon approximately keeps its near side toward Earth. High playback speeds are suited to orbital exploration; slow time to examine surface rotation.

Drag to orbit, pinch or scroll to zoom, and use two fingers or right-drag to pan. All orbital calculations and visual assets run locally after loading.
